Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GrokExpedia.us collects, uses, protects, and manages information when readers visit our website, use search, subscribe, or contact us.

By GrokExpedia Editorial Team • Updated 2026-05-04 • Privacy Policy

🔐 In a nutshell: We collect minimal data to run the site, improve content, and show ads. We never sell your personal information. You have control over cookies and email subscriptions. Read on for full details.

Introduction

GrokExpedia.us is an independent educational website focused on AI search, AI knowledge systems, digital research literacy, citations, source verification, and trustworthy publishing. We respect reader privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to operate the website, improve content, prevent abuse, communicate with readers, and support advertising or analytics. By using GrokExpedia.us, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information we may collect

We may collect limited information when you visit the website, use search, subscribe to updates, submit forms, or contact us.

Cookies and tracking technologies

GrokExpedia.us may use cookies, pixels, local storage, analytics tools, and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, measure traffic, prevent spam, and support advertising. Cookies may be placed by us or by third-party services such as analytics providers, advertising networks, embedded content providers, or security tools.

🍪 Your choice: You can usually disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, some website features may not work correctly if cookies are disabled. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

Advertising and third-party partners

GrokExpedia.us may display advertisements through third-party advertising networks. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and understand interactions with advertisements. Third-party advertisers may collect information directly through their own technologies. Their privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policies, not this Privacy Policy.

Newsletter and email communication

If you subscribe to our newsletter or submit your email address, we may use it to send updates, respond to messages, or provide requested information. You may unsubscribe from newsletter communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option in the email or by contacting us.

External links

GrokExpedia.us may link to external websites, research papers, official sources, social platforms, AI tools, and news publications. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or data handling policies of external websites. Readers should review the privacy policies of third-party websites before submitting personal information.

Data protection and security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure. No website, database, transmission method, or online service can guarantee complete security. Readers should avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, medical, or legal information through general contact forms.

Children's privacy

GrokExpedia.us is intended for general educational and informational audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Your privacy choices

  • 🍪 Disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • 📧 Unsubscribe from newsletter emails (link in every email).
  • ✉️ Contact us to request correction or deletion of information you submitted directly.
  • 🚫 Avoid submitting personal information through website forms.
  • ⚙️ Use browser privacy tools or ad preference settings provided by advertising networks.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for website operation, communication, analytics, legal compliance, security, and recordkeeping. Contact messages, newsletter information, logs, and analytics data may be retained for different periods depending on operational, legal, or technical needs.

International users

GrokExpedia.us may be accessed from different countries. By using the website, you understand that information may be processed in locations where our website, hosting, analytics, advertising, or service providers operate.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect website changes, legal requirements, analytics tools, advertising practices, or operational improvements. The updated date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last revised. Continued use of the website after updates means you accept the revised Privacy Policy.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy choices, data handling, or correction requests, please contact us through the website contact page.
