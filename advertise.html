Audience & reach

GrokExpedia.us serves readers who arrive with intent: they are researching AI search engines, learning to verify AI‑generated content, comparing knowledge platforms, or building trustworthy publications of their own. Our audience includes:

📊 AI researchers & developers 🎓 University faculty & students ✍️ Content publishers & journalists 📚 Information literacy advocates 🤖 AI tool builders

Traffic is primarily organic search from the US, Europe, and Asia, concentrated on evergreen explainers about citations, verification, and AI knowledge systems — topics with long shelf life and high reader trust. Detailed audience figures are shared with serious inquiries on request.

Editorial separation & brand safety

Editorial independence is non‑negotiable. Advertising does not influence our coverage, analysis, comparisons, or recommendations. All sponsored content is:

  • ✅ Clearly labeled “Sponsored” or “Advertisement”, visually separated from editorial content
  • ✅ Reviewed against our quality guidelines before it runs
  • ✅ Subject to our Editorial Policy and Corrections Policy

We do not accept advertising that promotes misinformation, unsubstantiated health or financial claims, hate speech, or illegal products. We reserve the right to decline any creative that conflicts with our mission of reader‑first AI literacy — and we exercise it.

Advertising formats we accept

  • Display ads – static or animated (GIF, HTML5); please keep file sizes lean for page speed.
  • Native / sponsored articles – written by your team or ours, subject to editorial approval and clear labeling.
  • Text links – contextual, with rel=“sponsored” attributes as required.
  • Video – non‑autoplay, placed outside critical content, with captions.

Pricing & how to get started

We keep pricing flexible: CPM (cost per thousand impressions), flat‑rate monthly placements, or custom sponsorship packages, depending on placement, duration, and volume. Because rates depend on current availability, we share a transparent rate card with qualified inquiries rather than publishing fixed prices that would quickly go stale.
