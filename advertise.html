Editorial separation & brand safety

We take editorial independence seriously. Advertising does not influence our coverage, analysis, or recommendations. All sponsored content is:

  • ✅ Clearly labeled with “Sponsored” or “Advertisement”
  • ✅ Reviewed to ensure it meets our quality guidelines
  • ✅ Subject to our Editorial Policy and Corrections Policy

We do not accept advertisements that promote misinformation, unsubstantiated health claims, hate speech, or illegal products. We reserve the right to decline any ad that conflicts with our mission of reader‑first AI literacy.

Advertising formats we accept

  • Display ads – static or animated (GIF, HTML5), max file size 150KB.
  • Native / sponsored articles – written by your team or our editorial staff (subject to approval).
  • Text links – contextual, no‑follow or sponsored attribute as required.
  • Video ads – non‑autoplay, mute‑friendly, placed outside critical content.
