📌 General questions

What is GrokExpedia?

GrokExpedia is an independent educational publication about AI knowledge systems — AI search engines, chatbots, citations, and AI‑generated encyclopedias like Grokipedia. We publish human‑edited guides, verification tutorials, and comparative analysis that help readers evaluate AI‑generated information before trusting it.

Are you affiliated with xAI, Grok, or Grokipedia?

No. GrokExpedia is independently operated and is not affiliated with xAI, Grok, Grokipedia, Wikipedia, or the Wikimedia Foundation. We cover these platforms as outside observers — which is exactly what lets us evaluate them critically.

Who is GrokExpedia for?

Students, researchers, educators, journalists, publishers, developers, and curious readers — anyone who encounters AI‑generated answers and wants the skills to check them. Beginners should start with our tutorials; researchers with the research hub.

✍️ Editorial & AI content

What type of content does GrokExpedia publish?

Educational guides, AI explainers, comparison articles, research summaries, verification tutorials, and documentation about AI search and digital knowledge systems. Everything is free to read, with no paywall. We do not publish financial, legal, or medical advice.

Do you use AI to produce content?

AI tools may assist with outlining, formatting, or research support, but every article is written, fact‑checked, and approved by human editors before publication. We never publish AI‑generated claims without verification. Our AI ethics policy documents the full rules.

How do you stay editorially independent?

We accept no payment for favorable coverage, and advertising — which is always labeled — never influences our analysis, comparisons, or corrections. Our editorial policy is public, and significant corrections are logged for readers to inspect.

✅ Verification & accuracy

How do you verify information?

We prioritize primary sources — official documentation, research papers, and established reporting — and cross‑check claims across multiple independent sources. Articles carry publication and update dates, link to their sources, and explicitly mark claims where evidence is thin or developing.

Can AI‑generated content contain mistakes?

Yes. AI search systems and AI‑generated encyclopedias can produce inaccurate, outdated, or misleading information stated with full confidence. Always verify important claims against original, independent sources. Our verification tutorials teach a repeatable workflow for doing exactly that.

How do you handle corrections?

Report errors via the contact page with the page URL, the incorrect statement, and a supporting source. An editor reviews every report; confirmed errors are fixed promptly, and substantive corrections are logged publicly under our corrections policy.

⚖️ Legal, privacy & advertising

Is your content professional advice?

No. Everything on GrokExpedia is for educational and informational purposes only — not legal, financial, medical, academic, or professional advice. Consult a qualified professional for decisions that affect your health, money, or legal rights.

What data do you collect, and do you show ads?

We collect minimal data — anonymous analytics and your email only if you subscribe to the newsletter — and we never sell personal information. Some pages show labeled, non‑intrusive ads to cover costs; ads never influence editorial content. Details: privacy policy, cookie policy.

📬 Contact & corrections

How can I report an issue, suggest a topic, or contact the team?

Use the contact page: choose “Editorial / correction” for errors (include the URL and a source), or describe your topic suggestion or partnership idea in the message. We reply to most messages within 24–48 hours on business days; contributor pitches take 5–7 days.
