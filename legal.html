🛡️ Privacy & compliance

We aim to collect as little data as possible while keeping the site fast, functional, and measurable. Key commitments:

  • Data minimization – only what is needed for site functionality and basic analytics.
  • No sale of personal data – we do not sell or rent user information to third parties.
  • GDPR / CCPA rights – EU and California residents may request access, correction, or deletion of their data via our contact page.
  • Third‑party services – analytics are privacy‑respecting; advertising partners are disclosed in the cookie policy.
