🛡️ Privacy & compliance

We are committed to maintaining transparency regarding data usage, analytics, cookies, and advertising practices. Users are encouraged to review our privacy and cookie policies to understand how information may be collected and processed while using the site.

  • Data minimization – We only collect data necessary for site functionality and analytics.
  • No sale of personal data – We do not sell or rent user information to third parties.
  • GDPR / CCPA rights – EU and California residents may request access, deletion, or correction of their data via our contact form.
  • Third-party services – We use anonymized analytics (e.g., Plausible or self-hosted) to respect privacy. Ad partners are disclosed in our cookie policy.
