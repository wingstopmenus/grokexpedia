📢 Report errors or outdated information

Accuracy is important to us. If you discover outdated, incorrect, or misleading information, please report it using the checklist below.

Include the following in your report:

  • The article or page URL
  • The issue or incorrect statement (quote directly if possible)
  • Your suggested correction
  • A trusted supporting source (e.g., official documentation, research paper, primary source)

❓ Frequently asked questions

What is GrokExpedia.us?

GrokExpedia.us is an independent educational platform focused on AI research, source verification, Grokipedia analysis, citations, and reader‑first AI literacy. We are not affiliated with xAI, Grok, or Wikipedia.

How do I verify a claim I read on GrokExpedia.us?

Each article includes source references, publication dates, and editorial notes. We encourage you to click through to primary sources and cross‑check with independent reporting. Our verification tutorials provide step‑by‑step guidance.

How can I suggest a new topic or correction?

Use our contact form or the correction checklist above. We review all submissions and update articles when appropriate.

Does GrokExpedia.us use AI to generate content?

Our editorial team writes and reviews all content. We may use AI tools for research or brainstorming, but every final article is human‑fact‑checked and source‑attributed.

How do I report a broken link or technical issue?

Please email support@grokexpedia.us or use the contact page. Include the page URL and a description of the issue.

📞 Need direct support?

For account questions, editorial inquiries, partnerships, advertising requests, or contributor support, please visit our Contact Us page. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
