A research workspace for AI knowledge

GrokExpedia works as an AI research workspace in the browser: search any topic, open detailed guides, follow citation trails back to primary sources, compare related knowledge pages, and keep your research organized. It is built for transparent, source‑aware learning — every claim is written to be checked, not just believed.

Each guide is structured the same way: the question, the evidence, the sources, and — where the evidence is thin — an explicit statement of what remains uncertain.

🛡️ Verify any claim — using our workflow

  1. Isolate the claim – write down the exact factual sentence you want to check.
  2. Follow the citation – open the linked source and confirm it actually supports the claim.
  3. Cross‑check – find 2–3 independent references saying the same thing.
  4. Flag uncertainty – if sources disagree, note the disagreement instead of picking a winner silently.
  5. Report it – found an error in our coverage? Tell us — sourced reports are fixed first.
