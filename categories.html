How to use this library

Follow the question, not the hype

Every topic here is written around a practical question. Wondering whether to trust an AI-generated article? Start with AI Ethics and AI Research. Trying to get your content cited by answer engines? AI Search Engines covers ranking factors, crawlers, and spam-free optimization. Curious about the technology itself? Machine Learning explains retrieval, embeddings, and knowledge graphs without assuming a technical background.

All guides are human-edited and source-aware: they show their reasoning, distinguish confirmed facts from open questions, and tell you what to verify independently. GrokExpedia is an independent publication — not affiliated with xAI, Grok, Grokipedia, Wikipedia, or the Wikimedia Foundation — and our only loyalty is to readers who want to understand AI knowledge systems clearly.

Editorial standards

What you'll find in every guide

Every guide in these libraries is written to the same standard: explain the concept plainly, show how it works with concrete examples, and be honest about limits. Where facts are confirmed, we say so; where the picture is still forming — as it often is with new AI products — we mark the uncertainty instead of filling it with speculation.

The guides also share a practical bias. Rather than describing AI systems in the abstract, each one answers a reader's real question: should I trust this answer, how do I check it, what should I build, what should I teach. Use the search box on any topic page to filter its guides, and follow the cross-links between topics — the ideas connect, and so do the libraries.
