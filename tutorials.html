📋 Step‑by‑step: verify any AI‑generated claim

  1. Extract the exact claim – write down the factual sentence you want to verify, word for word.
  2. Find the original source – look for hyperlinks, footnotes, or named references inside the AI output.
  3. Perform a lateral search – open 2–3 independent sources (news, academic, official) and compare what each says.
  4. Check timeliness – is the information still accurate? AI models may rely on outdated training data.
  5. Document uncertainty – if sources disagree, note the discrepancy instead of drawing a firm conclusion.
  6. Use our source checker – paste the URL or claim into the Source Checker tool for automated hints.

📋 Tutorial: cite AI‑generated content correctly

  1. Name the tool, model, and date – e.g., “Grok (xAI), queried 2026‑10‑07.” The date is part of the citation.
  2. Quote, don’t paraphrase loosely – copy the exact passage so readers can see what the model actually said.
  3. Add the underlying sources – if the AI cited sources, verify and cite those too; they carry more weight than the AI’s summary.
  4. Label AI assistance – in your own writing, note where AI helped (drafting, summarizing) versus where claims are yours and sourced.
  5. Keep a retrievable record – save the chat link or export; “an AI told me” is not verifiable.
