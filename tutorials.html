📋 Step‑by‑step: verify any AI‑generated claim

  1. Extract the exact claim – Write down the factual sentence you want to verify.
  2. Find the original source – Look for hyperlinks, footnotes, or named references inside the AI output.
  3. Perform a lateral search – Open 2–3 independent sources (news, academic, official) and compare.
  4. Check timeliness – Is the information still accurate? AI models may use outdated training data.
  5. Document uncertainty – If sources disagree, note the discrepancy and avoid drawing firm conclusions.
  6. Use our source checker – Paste the URL or claim into the Source Checker tool for automated hints.
