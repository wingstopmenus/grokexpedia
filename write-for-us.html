Write for GrokExpedia.us

Share useful, original, and well-researched articles about Grokipedia, Wikipedia, AI knowledge systems, citations, verification, and responsible digital publishing.

By GrokExpedia Editorial Team • Updated 2026-05-20 • Guest Post Guidelines

✅ Why write for GrokExpedia.us? Reach an engaged audience of AI researchers, educators, publishers, and fact‑checkers. Build your authority in the AI literacy space while helping readers navigate AI‑generated knowledge platforms.

Guest post submissions

GrokExpedia.us welcomes thoughtful guest contributions from writers, researchers, educators, technologists, editors, and digital publishing professionals who can explain complex knowledge systems in a clear, practical, and reader-first way.

We accept guest articles that help readers understand Grokipedia, Wikipedia, AI-generated knowledge platforms, AI search engines, citations, source verification, editorial trust, and online research literacy.

To submit a guest post idea, email us at contact@grokexpedia.us.

Topics we accept

📘 Grokipedia explainers & comparisons
🌐 Wikipedia & community encyclopedia models
🤖 AI knowledge platforms & research tools
🔍 AI search engines & answer engines
✅ Fact‑checking workflows for AI content
📖 Digital & media literacy
✍️ Editorial standards, corrections & attribution
⚖️ Comparisons: Grokipedia vs Wikipedia vs traditional search

Google-safe guest post policy

GrokExpedia.us follows a reader-first guest post policy. Guest posts must be created to inform, educate, explain, or analyze. We do not accept articles created mainly for artificial link building, paid link placement, keyword manipulation, or site reputation abuse.

  • Guest posts must provide real editorial value for GrokExpedia readers.
  • Promotional, affiliate-heavy, casino, adult, gambling, crypto spam, illegal, or irrelevant submissions are not accepted.
  • Paid, sponsored, affiliate, or compensated links must be disclosed and may use appropriate link attributes such as rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow".
  • User-submitted links may be edited, removed, nofollowed, or rejected at our discretion.
  • Exact-match anchor text created mainly for SEO manipulation is not accepted.
  • We reserve the right to reject, edit, update, or remove any guest post that does not meet our standards.

Article quality guidelines

📏 900‑2,000 words (depending on topic)
📑 Short paragraphs & helpful headings
💡 Practical examples, checklists, workflows
🔗 Credible references (docs, papers, journalism)
⚠️ No exaggerated claims or fake stats
🤖 AI‑assisted content must be human‑edited & verified

What we do not accept

  • Copied, plagiarized, spun, or lightly rewritten content.
  • Articles written only to place backlinks.
  • Content unrelated to Grokipedia, Wikipedia, AI knowledge systems, search, citations, or verification.
  • False claims, fake author identities, fake expertise, or fabricated sources.
  • Overly promotional product reviews or brand advertisements disguised as education.
  • Content that violates copyright, privacy, safety, or applicable laws.

How to submit

  1. Write your pitch or completed draft.
  2. Email to contact@grokexpedia.us with subject line: Guest Post Submission - GrokExpedia.
  3. Include: proposed title, short summary, author name, bio, relevant experience, and sources/references.

Editorial review process

Every submission is reviewed for relevance, originality, source quality, accuracy, structure, readability, and compliance with our editorial policy. Acceptance is not guaranteed.

Our editorial team may edit accepted posts for clarity, formatting, accuracy, internal linking, disclosure, compliance, and reader experience.
