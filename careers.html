Careers at GrokExpedia

We are interested in writers, editors, researchers, and builders who care about source-aware AI knowledge.

By GrokExpedia Editorial Team • Updated 2026-10-07

Join our mission: trustworthy AI knowledge for everyone

GrokExpedia is a small, independent publication with an outsized standard: every guide accurate, every claim checkable, every correction public. We’re always interested in connecting with writers, researchers, editors, and builders who share that standard — whether for freelance contributions now or roles we create later.

If you believe AI literacy and source transparency should be accessible to everyone, we’d like to hear from you.

Our team values

🔍 Verification‑first

Every claim must be traceable to a reliable source.

🧠 Reader‑first clarity

Plain language, structured content, useful examples.

🤝 Open collaboration

Respectful feedback and shared editorial standards.

⚙️ SEO with integrity

Optimize for discovery, never for manipulation.

Current opportunities

We are not actively hiring full‑time employees right now. Instead, we maintain a talent pool for future roles and work regularly with freelance contributors. Areas we’re building toward:

  • Editorial research – fact‑checking, source validation, topic scoping.
  • Technical writing – documentation, API guides, AI workflow explainers.
  • SEO operations – keyword research, on‑page optimization, performance analysis.
  • AI‑assisted publishing – workflows blending human editing with generative tools.
  • Content strategy & product design – structuring knowledge hubs and reader journeys.

We also welcome freelance contributors, subject‑matter specialists, and experienced digital writers who can produce clear, trustworthy, search‑optimized content.

What we look for

Strong candidates value factual accuracy, reader intent, careful sourcing, plain‑language communication, and concrete real‑world examples. Experience with research writing, structured content, and digital publishing workflows matters more to us than credentials — a portfolio of well‑sourced articles (or a background in journalism, technical writing, or library science) tells us what we need to know.

We especially value people who can balance human readability with modern search optimization — writing that both readers and search engines can trust.

Contributor program

The fastest way to work with us is the contributor program: review the contributor guidelines, then submit a focused, original article proposal. Strong submissions lead to published guest pieces — and, over time, to ongoing contributor relationships.

📝 How to submit a proposal:
  1. Read our editorial policy and style guide.
  2. Choose a topic related to AI search, verification, Grokipedia, or digital literacy.
  3. Write a 300‑word outline with 3–5 sources you plan to cite.
  4. Send it to contributions@grokexpedia.us with the subject line “Contributor proposal: [Your topic]”.

Why work with GrokExpedia?

  • 📚 Impact – your work helps thousands of readers make better‑informed decisions about AI tools.
  • 🌱 Growth – mentorship, detailed editorial feedback, and resources to sharpen your research and writing.
  • 🤝 Flexibility – remote‑first, asynchronous collaboration with freelance contributors.
  • 🛠️ Tools & support – access to our source‑verification checklists and editorial workflow.
  • 🏆 Recognition – real bylines, contributor spotlights, and shared credit on major projects.

How we evaluate interest

Every message to our careers inbox is read by an editor — not filtered by software. We acknowledge receipt, review your background and samples against current and anticipated needs, and keep promising profiles on file for six months. If nothing fits today, a strong contributor proposal is the best way to start the relationship: published work with us carries more weight than any résumé line.

✉️ Express your interest

Send us:

  • A brief introduction — 2–3 paragraphs on your background and interest in AI literacy.
  • Your résumé or LinkedIn profile.
  • 2–3 writing samples or links to published work (if available).

Email careers@grokexpedia.us — we keep your information on file for six months and reach out when a relevant opportunity arises.
